"Sweet Crumbs Bakery"

A customer-success storyboard following Ankita, a bakery owner, as a Customer Success Manager (CSM) helps her turn unsold stock into loyal customers. Tap any scene to enlarge it.

1

The Struggle

Inside Ankita's bakery, surrounded by unsold items and empty tables. She is overwhelmed and stressed by inventory issues, sales and high waste.

CSM (via phone): "Let's work together to find a solution."
2

Identifying the Problem

A video call between Ankita and the CSM. Together they find the root causes: inconsistent inventory, limited customer insights and a lack of marketing.

CSM: "Let's take a closer look at your sales patterns and see where we can make adjustments."
3

Implementing Solutions

In the bakery and back office, the CSM shows Ankita a simple inventory management system, suggests new marketing strategies and sets up a loyalty program for returning customers.

Ankita: "This system is so easy to use, I can finally keep track of what's selling and what's not."
4

Achieving Success

The bakery is full of customers and Ankita is happily serving them. Sales are up, waste is down and she thanks the CSM for their ongoing support.

Ankita: "My bakery is finally thriving and I have loyal customers coming back every week."

Continued Growth

The bakery keeps growing as Ankita expands her menu and improves her marketing, with ongoing support from the CSM.
